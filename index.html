About

A small kernel with room for different operating systems.

Orfreon explores the exokernel model, which limits the kernel to allocating hardware resources and enforcing protection boundaries. Higher-level operating system abstractions live in a Library OS (LibOS).

By separating resource protection from operating system policy, the design aims to let different LibOS implementations choose their own abstractions while sharing a common kernel foundation.

Vision

Core principles

  • Minimal Core

    Keep kernel responsibilities focused on the mechanisms needed to share hardware.

  • Resource Protection

    Make controlled resource allocation and isolation explicit design goals.

  • LibOS Flexibility

    Give Library OS implementations room to choose their own policies and abstractions.

  • Open Development

    Share design decisions and development progress as the project takes shape.
